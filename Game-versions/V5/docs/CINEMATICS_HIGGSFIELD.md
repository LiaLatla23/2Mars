# Cinematics and animation · 2Mars V5.1

## In-game launch video

The V5 project includes `video/launch.ogv`, used in the launch sequence. The clip is integrated as a game asset and is separate from the native Godot animations used elsewhere in the mission flow.

## Production attribution

Team SpaceNuts confirms that Higgsfield was used for the launch animation and additional rover animation work. Refer to [AI and asset attribution](../../../evidence/AI_AND_ASSET_ATTRIBUTION.md) for the wider production-tool record.

Generated images and video should follow the current NASA Space Apps rules for visible AI/SI disclosure. The repository's attribution note records the tool use; the final exported media itself must be inspected to verify its visible marking.

## In-game fallback and scope

The game uses its own native animation systems for mission presentation. The presence of the launch clip does not mean that every phase of cruise, entry, descent or landing is generated video or a physically exact reproduction of Mars 2020.

The landing and mission sequence are simplified educational representations, not a real mission reconstruction.
