# Research & Resources

This directory contains the research, scientific references, technical documentation, and multimedia resources used to support the development of **ToMars**.

The purpose of this research is to provide a reliable scientific and engineering foundation for the game, which focuses on designing and operating a robotic rover mission on Mars.

## Research Areas

Our research is organized into the following areas:

- **Mission Design:** Mission objectives, planning, requirements, constraints, and engineering decisions.
- **Mars Environment:** Martian geology, atmosphere, terrain, and environmental conditions.
- **Rover Technology:** Rover architecture, mobility, power systems, communications, and navigation.
- **Scientific Instruments:** Instruments used to study Martian rocks, soil, and environment.
- **Scientific Exploration:** Perseverance's science objectives, sample collection, and exploration activities.
- **Visual Resources:** NASA RAW images, maps, videos, and other multimedia materials.

## Research Principles

- Prioritize official and scientifically reliable sources, especially NASA and JPL.
- Preserve original source links and attribution.
- Distinguish verified scientific information from game interpretations and simplifications.
- Document how relevant research contributes to the game's mechanics, systems, and educational content.
