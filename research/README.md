# Research and resources

This directory contains scientific and technical research supporting **2Mars V5.1**, an educational Mars mission design and exploration game by Team SpaceNuts for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

Research informs mission concepts, rover systems, scientific instruments, communications, sample investigations and mission-planning explanations. It also documents where the game uses simplified models rather than real mission calculations.

## Research areas

- **Mission design:** objectives, launch concepts, mission architecture, constraints and engineering trade-offs.
- **Mars environment:** geology, atmosphere, terrain and environmental conditions.
- **Rover technology:** mobility, power, communications, navigation and rover subsystems.
- **Scientific instruments:** instruments used to study Martian rocks, soil and environment.
- **Scientific exploration:** Perseverance's science objectives, sample collection and exploration activities.
- **Scientific traceability:** how a source informs a game fact, mechanic, player decision or consequence.

## Research principles

- Prioritize original and reliable sources, especially NASA and NASA/JPL.
- Preserve source links and attribution.
- Distinguish verified real-world information from game parameters, fictional content and educational simplifications.
- Do not imply that a resource is integrated into the game merely because it appears in the research inventory.
- Document the connection between sources and implemented mechanics.
