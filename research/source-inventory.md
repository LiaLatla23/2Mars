# Source Inventory

This document records research resources consulted or identified during the development of **2Mars**. It is a broader research inventory, not a list of datasets or assets embedded in the game.

For resources confirmed in the reviewed V4 game, see [NASA_RESOURCES_USED.md](NASA_RESOURCES_USED.md). That document distinguishes in-game NASA/JPL sources from background research and resources that were not integrated.

## 3. Interpretation of status

- **Integrated; in-game source:** listed in the V4 Science Library and connected to facts, stories, or mechanics.
- **Research reference:** identified or consulted by the team, but not identified as a source directly integrated into V4.
- **Not integrated as game assets/data:** the resource may inform research, but its imagery, maps, datasets, or software are not embedded in the reviewed game.

The V4 audit found no NASA planetary datasets (such as PDS, MRO, Curiosity, or InSight data) loaded directly by the game. The V4 visuals were not identified as NASA imagery or maps.
