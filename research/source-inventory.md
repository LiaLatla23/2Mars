## 4. Research Status

The status labels indicate the team's progress in reviewing each resource.

- **Identified:** The resource has been located and registered.
- **Reviewed:** Relevant information has been examined.
- **Extracted:** Useful information has been documented.
- **Applied:** The information has been incorporated into the game's design or development.

## 5. Research Documentation Guidelines

For every new resource added to this inventory:

1. Use the original source whenever possible.
2. Record the organization or author.
3. Identify the specific research area.
4. Explain how the resource contributes to ToMars.
5. Update its status as the team reviews and applies the information.
6. Preserve the original link and attribution.
